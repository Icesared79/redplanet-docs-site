# redplanet-docs-site — CC Rules

The docs site for docs.redplanetdata.com. Unlike `signal-app`, this repo DOES deploy
on a push to `main` through its Vercel Git integration — verify a deploy by evidence,
never by assumption. Session coordination, worktree and commit-hygiene rules follow the
global `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`; this repo's lock name in `atlas_cc_task_locks` is
`redplanet-docs-site`.

## Paul's files — where anything he saved actually is

Paul's files: anything Paul downloads or exports on his laptop lands in `C:\Inbox`
on atlas-mini (his laptop's `R:` drive). When Paul refers to a file he saved,
downloaded, exported or provided, look in `C:\Inbox` first, including subfolders,
matching by name and checking the newest files, then `C:\Users\Atlas\Downloads`. If
it is in neither place, list the ten newest items in `C:\Inbox` with their times
instead of asking Paul where it is. Never move, rename or delete Paul's files there;
copy what you need into your worktree, and do not leave your own working files in
`C:\Inbox`.
